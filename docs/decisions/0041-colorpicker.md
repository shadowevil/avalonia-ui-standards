# 0041 — ColorPicker and ColorView

- **Status:** Proposed, 2026-11-13
- **Decision:**
  - The controls come from Avalonia's separate package `Avalonia.Controls.ColorPicker`, pinned at 12.1.6 (the version matching Avalonia 12.1.6) in `Directory.Packages.props` ([0008](0008-target-versions.md), [0010](0010-solution-layout.md)). The library references it, so apps get it with the library.
  - `StandardsTheme` adds the package's Fluent styles (`avares://Avalonia.Controls.ColorPicker/Themes/Fluent/Fluent.xaml`) as a child right after `FluentTheme`, as Fluent itself is a child, and the Blocks themes for ColorPicker, ColorView, ColorSpectrum, ColorSlider and ColorPreviewer override the package's ([0017](0017-app-usage.md): no extra step for apps).
    - **Why:** The package's own resources (the checkerboard brush, its converters) stay available to apps and to the library's templates, and the library's themes win because the theme's own resources are looked up before its children.
  - All five are Blocks ([0004](0004-tier-names.md)), restyled Fluent ([0003](0003-restyle-fluent.md)) with the library's own templates. Fluent's part and element names are kept (`PART_HexTextBox`, `PART_TabControl`, the spectrum's `PART_LayoutRoot`, `PART_SizingPanel`, `PART_SpectrumRectangle`, `PART_SpectrumOverlayRectangle`, `PART_SpectrumEllipse`, `PART_SpectrumOverlayEllipse`, `PART_InputTarget`, `PART_SelectionEllipsePanel`, the sliders' `PART_Track`, `PART_DecreaseButton`, `PART_IncreaseButton`, the previewer's four `PART_Accent…Border`s, and `ColorSpectrum`, `Component1Slider` and the other named sliders and inputs), so the controls' code and app styles find them.
    - **Why:** The colour logic (spectrum bitmaps, slider gradients, hex parsing, accent steps) stays the package's; only the look changes.
  - **The colour chip.** Every shown colour (the picker's chip, palette colours, the previewer) is a squircle over Fluent's checkerboard (`ColorControlCheckeredBackgroundBrush`) with a 1 px hairline edge in the Slider thumb's edge colour (black 12 %).
    - **Why:** The checkerboard shows transparency, as in Fluent. A translucent dark edge separates a colour from a surface of the same colour (a white chip on a white field) on any colour and in both themes, and it is a value the library already has.
  - **The ColorPicker field.** The closed ColorPicker is drawn as the closed ComboBox field ([0028](0028-combobox.md)): a `SquircleBorder` `PART_BorderElement` with `CornerRadiusControl` 8, the density height (32 Normal, 24 Compact), a 1 px border, Fluent's ComboBox colours, no pressed look, and a square caret zone with `Icons.CaretDown` at 16 px in `ComboBoxDropDownGlyphForeground`. The chip fills the rest, sized and inset as a field button ([0027](0027-text-input.md)): 24 px high at Normal and 20 at Compact, inset 4 or 2, corner radius 4 or 6. Fluent's minimum width 64 is kept. An app's `Content` replaces the chip, as in Fluent. Keyboard focus shows the focus ring ([0019](0019-focus-ring.md)); pointer focus shows nothing.
    - **Why:** The picker shows a value and opens a chooser for it, as ComboBox, DatePicker and CalendarDatePicker do, so it takes the field look they share rather than the DropDownButton's Standard button look (Fluent's, and WinUI's sample) or Fluent's chevron. The field-button inset and radius make the chip concentric with the field, like the clear and spin buttons.
  - **The flyout.** The picker's button is Fluent's `DropDownButton` with a `Flyout` holding the ColorView, so its presenter is the library's glass flyout ([0036](0036-menus-and-flyouts.md)): `CornerRadiusOverlay` 16, `SpacingL` padding, the window's overlay layer, the `MotionFast` fade in (none under reduce motion). It opens below the field, left edges aligned, `SpacingXS` apart; Avalonia flips it when there is no room. Fluent's zero-padding class and its placement above the field are dropped.
    - **Why:** It is the glass overlay rule of 0036, and below the field is where the ComboBox and CalendarDatePicker drop-downs open, so the field's overlays open in one place.
  - **The ColorView.** Tabs over pages, then the previewer. The view has no surface of its own; it sits on its container (the flyout's glass, or a panel), as tab content does ([0033](0033-tabs-and-expander.md)).
    - The mode switch (spectrum, palette, components) is Fluent's `TabControl` with the library's tab look: icon headers (`Icons.Drop`, `Icons.SquaresFour`, `Icons.SlidersHorizontal`) at the Icon density size, the sliding accent underline and the hairline, left-aligned. Each tab has a tooltip and an automation name ("Spectrum", "Palette", "Components"). Fluent's grey tab band and equal-width tabs are dropped.
      - **Why:** 0033 keeps tabs for switching between content areas and the segmented control for choosing a value of a setting ([0032](0032-segmented-control.md)); the three modes are three views of the same colour, not a setting. Icons, as in Fluent and macOS's colour panel, keep the strip short; an icon alone does not name the view, so the tooltip and automation name do (the 0032 icon-segment rule).
    - Every page is 256 px high (the spectrum's side), `SpacingM` below the strip, and the view's width is the spectrum plus a vertical slider either side, `SpacingM` apart: 320 at Normal, 312 at Compact (the thumbs are 20 and 16). Fluent's fixed 350 × 338 is dropped.
      - **Why:** Switching tabs never changes the view's size, and the sizes sit on the 4 px grid ([0013](0013-spacing-and-density.md)).
    - The spectrum page: the third-component slider, the spectrum and the alpha slider, `SpacingM` apart, as in Fluent.
    - The palette page: the library ListBox (padding `SpacingXS`) over Fluent's palette, in a uniform grid of the palette's columns. Each colour is a cell holding its chip `SpacingXS` in, with the chip's corner radius the cell's less that inset (4). Hover and press are the list item fills behind the chip (`ListMedium`, `BaseMediumLow`, [0024](0024-lists-and-scrolling.md)); the selected cell has a 2 px accent ring (`AccentFillColor`) on its edge with a 2 px gap to the chip; keyboard focus shows the focus ring outside the cell. Each chip has the colour's display name as its tooltip and automation name, as in Fluent.
      - **Why:** The list item fills are the library's hover and press. A ring rather than a fill marks the selection because a fill would cover the colour. The ring sits inside the cell and the focus ring outside it, so selection and keyboard focus on the same cell read as two marks, not one ring drawn twice. Fluent's contrast-coloured border is dropped for the accent.
    - The components page: the colour model as a SegmentedControl ("RGB", "HSV") and the hex value as a TextInput with a `#` in its side padding, `SpacingXS` before the text, side by side; then one row per component: its letter (R, G, B or H, S, V, then A), a NumericUpDown 64 px wide with no spin buttons (as in Fluent), and the component's colour slider. The letters and the `#` are in the placeholder colour (`TextControlPlaceholderForeground`), and the disabled text colour when disabled. Rows are `SpacingM` apart, from the top of the page.
      - **Why:** The model is a setting with two values, the case for the segmented control (0032). The inputs are the library's field Blocks, so they line up and show focus like every other field. Fluent's joined label boxes with squared corners are dropped: the letters label the fields as text, and the fields keep their own squircles.
  - **The spectrum.** The box spectrum is a squircle with `CornerRadiusControl` 8 and the hairline edge; the ring shape keeps Fluent's ellipse, with the same edge. The selector is not clipped, so it shows in full at the edges.
  - **The colour thumb.** The spectrum's selector and every ColorSlider's thumb are one design: the Slider thumb's size (20 Normal, 16 Compact, [0031](0031-slider-and-progress.md)) and shadow (0 1 3, black 25 %), drawn as a white ring 2 px wide between two 1 px hairline edges (black 12 %), with an open centre that shows the colour under it. It grows 10 % on hover and 20 % while pressed over `MotionFast`, as the Slider thumb does. Disabled, the ring takes the Slider's disabled thumb colour (`SystemControlBackgroundBaseLowBrush`). Fluent's large selector under a touch press (48 px) is kept. Keyboard focus shows the focus ring around the thumb.
    - **Why:** A solid white thumb, as on the Slider, would hide the colour it picks; a ring is the colour selector on macOS, iOS and WinUI. The edges and shadow make the white ring read on white as well as on dark colours, so Fluent's switch between a black and a white selector by luminance is dropped. The focus ring goes around the thumb because the arrow keys move it, as on the Slider.
  - **The colour sliders.** A ColorSlider's track is a full pill as thick as the thumb (20 or 16), showing the slider's gradient over the checkerboard with the hairline edge; the thumb runs along it.
    - **Why:** The gradient is the slider's information, so the track must be thick enough to show it; 0031's 4 px track is a line for a value, not a colour. The pill shape is 0031's. As thick as the thumb is Fluent's proportion, and the thumb's hover growth then shows past the track.
  - **The previewer.** The colour as a chip with `CornerRadiusControl` 8, as high as a control row (32 or 24), with Fluent's four accent chips (two darker, two lighter; clicking one picks it) as square chips either side, `SpacingXS` apart. Fluent's drop shadow is dropped.
    - **Why:** Chips are the library's colour swatch; a row height lines the previewer up with the inputs above it. Shadows in the library mark things to grab (thumbs), and a preview is not one.
  - Motion: the field's chrome, the palette cells and the thumbs use the button family's `MotionFast` transitions, none under reduce motion ([0016](0016-motion.md)); the tabs and the segmented control keep their own motion.
  - The catalog's ColorPicker page uses `Icons.Eyedropper` (Phosphor `eyedropper`), added under the rule of [0026](0026-icon.md).
- **Known for later:** A disabled picker keeps its chip at full colour (the field, border and caret take their disabled colours); dimming the colour would need a value the library does not have ([0007](0007-restyle-scope.md)). The component inputs and tab tooltips use English names, as Fluent's automation names are English.
- **Why:** The colour controls join the field, tab, list and slider families: the same field, overlay, focus ring and motion, with the colour logic left to the package.
