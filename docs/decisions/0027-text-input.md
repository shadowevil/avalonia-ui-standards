# 0027 — TextInput

- **Status:** Proposed, 2026-11-13
- **Decision:**
  - TextInput is Fluent's `TextBox`, restyled ([0003](0003-restyle-fluent.md)) with the library's own template. It keeps Fluent's structure and part names (`PART_BorderElement`, `PART_InnerDockPanel`, `PART_FloatingPlaceholder`, `PART_ScrollViewer`, `PART_Placeholder`, `PART_TextPresenter`), the `InnerLeftContent` and `InnerRightContent` slots, the `DataValidationErrors` root, the `.clearButton` and `.revealPasswordButton` classes and Fluent's context menus.
    - **Why:** Behaviour, keyboard handling and validation stay Fluent's; apps and Fluent controls that look for these parts still find them.
  - The chrome `PART_BorderElement` is a `SquircleBorder` with `CornerRadiusControl` 8 ([0014](0014-shape.md)).
    - **Why:** Fields share the shape of every other control.
  - Size by density ([0013](0013-spacing-and-density.md)), the same as Button ([0018](0018-button.md)): height 32 and padding 12,4 at Normal, height 24 and padding 8,2 at Compact, replacing Fluent's 10,6,6,5 and 4,2,4,2. Single-line text is vertically centred; with `AcceptsReturn` the text starts at the top padding. Fluent's minimum width (`TextControlThemeMinWidth`) is kept.
    - **Why:** Fields line up with buttons in a row and sit on the grid; centring keeps single-line text in the middle whatever the font's line height.
  - Colours are Fluent's TextControl keys, per state: `TextControl{Background,BorderBrush,Foreground}` at rest and with `PointerOver`, `Focused` and `Disabled`. Focus is any focus (pointer or keyboard).
    - **Why:** The same rule as Button: Fluent's own keys, fed by the library palette, so no new values ([0007](0007-restyle-scope.md)). The rest border (`BaseMedium`, AA-derived in [0011](0011-colour.md)) meets the 3:1 non-text contrast a field's boundary needs (WCAG 1.4.11) on the page, the card and the glass panel; a lighter hairline grey (`BaseLow`) would not (about 1.5:1). This is tested.
  - The border is 1 px in every state. On focus it turns accent (`TextControlBorderBrushFocused`); keyboard focus adds the focus ring ([0019](0019-focus-ring.md)). There is no second focus cue: no 2 px focus border and no bottom accent line.
    - **Why:** One focus cue family across the library: the accent border marks where typing goes, the ring marks keyboard focus as on every Block. A constant width means the text never shifts on focus, as it does with Fluent's 2 px focus border.
  - Error (`:error`) turns the border the danger colour (`DangerColor`, [0011](0011-colour.md)), over any other state's border.
    - **Why:** It ties errors to the library's danger colour, and it still shows while the field is focused, which is when the user fixes it. It meets 3:1 on every surface (tested).
  - The placeholder uses `TextControlPlaceholderForeground` (`BaseMedium`) at full opacity in every enabled state, and the disabled text colour when disabled. Fluent's 0.5 placeholder opacity and its lighter focused placeholder are dropped.
    - **Why:** Placeholder text is text: it meets 4.5:1 over the field fill on the page, the card and the glass panel (tested). At Fluent's 0.5 opacity it falls to about 2:1.
  - Selected text uses the AA accent fill (`AccentFillColor`) with white text (`AccentButtonForeground`), instead of the raw accent.
    - **Why:** The same selection as list items ([0024](0024-lists-and-scrolling.md)); the raw accent `#007CEA` gives 4.3:1 with white text, the AA fill 4.58:1.
  - Clear (`.clearButton`) and reveal password (`.revealPasswordButton`) are template parts, not `InnerRightContent`: subtle icon buttons with `Icons.XCircle` and `Icons.Eye` (password hidden) or `Icons.EyeSlash` (password shown), 16 px icons. As in Fluent, neither applies to multi-line or read-only text; clear shows while the field is focused and not empty, reveal while it is not empty.
    - **Why:** Apps keep `InnerRightContent` for their own content. The eye shows the action it takes, as in Windows and most web forms.
  - The buttons take their place in the layout as soon as their class is set, transparent and click-through, and fade in and out over `MotionFast` ([0016](0016-motion.md)); they are instant under reduce motion.
    - **Why:** The text never reflows when a button appears, and the fade matches the library's other state changes.
  - Field buttons (shared by every field Block): 24 px at Normal and 20 px at Compact, centred in the field height with an equal inset on every side (4 or 2), corner radius 8 less the inset (4 or 6) so the curves are concentric with the field. The first sits 4 px (`SpacingXS`) after the text. Colours are the Subtle button's ([0018](0018-button.md)): no fill at rest with Fluent's `TextControlButtonForeground`, then Standard's hover and pressed fills. No pressed scale. They never take keyboard focus (Fluent's are not focusable), so they have no ring.
    - **Why:** They read as members of the button family, and the field keeps the keyboard focus while they are used. Fluent's accent-on-hover look is dropped for the Subtle look the library already has.
  - Fades: the chrome's background and border cross-fade over `MotionFast` (`CrossFadeBrushTransition`, [0016](0016-motion.md)), instant under reduce motion.
  - A TextBox inside another field draws no chrome, fill, state or ring of its own (an inner theme); the field around it draws them. Fluent's CalendarDatePicker, not yet restyled, gets this inner theme on its TextBox and otherwise stays Fluent.
    - **Why:** Fluent sets that inner TextBox's transparent fill at template priority, below the TextInput state styles, so it would otherwise show TextInput's hover, focus and ring inside Fluent's own field.
- **Known for later steps:** Fluent's `DataValidationErrors` message under a field in error keeps Fluent's error text colour (`SystemControlErrorTextForegroundBrush`), which is yellow in dark and is not restyled here. `MaskedTextBox` keeps Fluent's theme.
- **Why:** A text field that lines up with buttons, keeps every Fluent behaviour, shows focus and errors in the library's colours, and stays legible and accessible in both themes.
